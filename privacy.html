Information handled by this website

Datapath is owned and operated by Hussain Basha Mulla. No account is required to use the website, and Datapath does not provide a form for submitting information. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond.

Storage in your browser

Datapath stores lesson progress and your light or dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. This lets the site remember these choices on that browser. The app uses the storage keys datapath_progress_v1 and datapath_theme. This information is not sent to a Datapath server by the site code. You can clear it using your browser's site-data settings.

Third-party fonts

The site currently loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request data. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's handling of data.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current Datapath website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or analytics tools. It may use third-party services that process technical information when a visitor accesses the site.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, that service and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize advertisements, depending on the visitor's settings and applicable requirements. Visitors can learn more about Google's advertising practices in Google's Advertising Privacy and Terms. This policy will be updated to describe the services actually used on the site.
