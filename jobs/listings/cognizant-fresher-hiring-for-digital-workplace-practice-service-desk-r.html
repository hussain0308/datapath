About this opportunity

Role Overview

This entry-level position focuses on delivering first-line (Level 1) troubleshooting, managing technical incidents, and ensuring minimal downtime for end-user computing environments. You will handle requests through multi-channel platforms (such as phone, chat, or self-service portals) while maintaining high customer satisfaction and adhering to service level agreements (SLAs).

Key Responsibilities

• Incident Management: Log, track, and manage IT support tickets accurately from creation to resolution within defined SLA timelines.
• Technical Troubleshooting: Diagnose and resolve basic issues related to hardware, software, Windows operating systems, peripheral devices (like printers), and connectivity.
• Access & Collaboration Support: Assist users with access-related services, password resets, multi-factor authentication (MFA), and collaboration tools like Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Teams.
• Issue Escalation: Coordinate and escalate complex technical problems or critical incidents to specialized internal IT teams or third-party vendors when necessary.
• Knowledge Documentation: Create, update, and maintain knowledge base (KB) articles and support documentation to enhance self-service adoption and accelerate future resolutions.
• Operational Shift Support: Deliver continuous support while working in a fast-paced, rotational shift environment across designated Cognizant office locations.
